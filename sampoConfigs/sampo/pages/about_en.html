About NuMAD

NuMAD Belgium

NuMAD Belgium is a collaboration between Ghent University's Ancient History research group, the Royal Library of Belgium (KBR), and the Ghent Centre for Digital Humanities. The project is building a database of coin finds in Belgium from Antiquity to the early Middle Ages, based on an inventory compiled at KBR. Read more on the research project page.

Hosting and support

This portal is hosted and supported by the Ghent Centre for Digital Humanities (GhentCDH). The technical implementation of NuMAD was carried out by GhentCDH.

Technical credits

NuMAD is based on Sampo-UI, an open-source framework developed by the Semantic Computing Research Group (SeCo) at Aalto University and the University of Helsinki. More information is available on the Sampo-UI project page. This implementation includes major contributions from GhentCDH.
